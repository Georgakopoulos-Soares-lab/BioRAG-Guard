# Evaluation

Generated by `bioguard eval`. Every number on this page came from a run; nothing
here is quoted from a paper or estimated.

## What this page is, and is not

The table below is the harness running against the **synthetic corpus shipped
with the package** (600 records, 4 cell types, 6 donors, 16 dimensions). It
demonstrates that the layers behave as specified and that the harness works.

It is **not** a replication of the research results. Those were measured on a
SCimilarity `model_v1.1` reference of 7,913,892 records with GSE136831 lung
queries, and reproducing them needs that atlas, the query set, and hours of
compute per arm. The research numbers are quoted in `docs/two-modes.md` and
attributed there; they are not reproduced here and must not be read off this
page.

## Reading the detection rates

A detection rate without its false-positive cost is not actionable, so the
budget appears in every row of the harness output. On a corpus this separable
the layers saturate; on a real atlas they will not, and the arms the research
found hardest -- the genuinely paraphrased protein poison at 0.21 capture, and
every in-place edit -- are the ones to watch.
